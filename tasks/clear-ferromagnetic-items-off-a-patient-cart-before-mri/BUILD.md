# Building the props

All items are ordinary purchases except the cart top, the cradle chocks and the sign; no drawings needed beyond these
sizes. Sizes marked "about" are estimates of a typical item, not measurements of a specific one.

| Prop | Size / material | Approx. cost |
|---|---|---|
| Cart top | stand-in for the top shelf of an MR Conditional cart, 84 x 38 cm, 2 cm thick: a 3 mm white plastic sheet (HDPE or acrylic) glued over a 17 mm extruded polystyrene foam board, plastic or glued joints only, no metal screws or brackets; red tape band 3 cm wide across the front edge | 30-60 USD |
| Sign | yellow board 36 x 20 cm on a plastic stand; red band across the top with DANGER in white, then MR ZONE IV (5 cm letters) and MAGNET ON in black, printed | 10 USD |
| Tray | red plastic tray 20 x 72 cm, 4 cm walls (or a bin of that size): the designated safe side | 15 USD |
| Oxygen cylinder | empty steel M6 / B-size cylinder (not aluminium), 6.4 cm diameter, about 29 cm with valve, about 1.3 kg (valve closed and capped; e.g. a retired medical one) | 40-100 USD |
| Cradle chocks | four 1.4 x 2 x 2 cm plastic or wood blocks glued to the shelf, two on each side of the cylinder at 6.5 cm either side of its middle | 1 USD |
| Trauma shears | 19 cm, orange plastic handles, blades that hold the magnet (not every stainless grade does: check before buying) | 8 USD |
| Adjustable wrench | plain steel 6 in (15 cm) adjustable wrench, no plastic grip; head about 5 cm wide, about 0.2 kg | 10 USD |
| Water bottle | 500 ml plastic bottle, 6.6 cm diameter, 20 cm tall, filled; plastic cap | 1 USD |
| Clipboard | all-plastic clipboard 23 x 31 cm (plastic clip, no steel spring) with a paper chart, no staples or paper clips | 5 USD |
| Emesis basin | plastic kidney dish, about 11 x 20 cm | 2 USD |

## MR status of the props

- **Cart.** A real MRI suite uses a cart labelled MR Conditional. One example: the AliMed MRI Nonmagnetic Utility Cart,
  https://www.alimed.com/products/mri-nonmagnetic-utility-cart (checked: the page loads and states "MR-conditional to
  3Tesla" and a size of 24"W x 24"H x 18"D). The arms work at bench height, so trials use the stand-in top above
  rather than a whole cart. It is plastic and foam only, with no metal, which are the materials ASTM F2503 describes
  for an MR Safe item (nonconducting, nonmetallic, nonmagnetic).
- **Magnet check, before every trial.** Touch a handheld magnet (any fridge or tool-pickup magnet) to each prop. It must
  cling to the cylinder body, the shears' blades and the wrench, and must not cling anywhere on the cart top, sign,
  tray, bottle, clipboard or basin. A prop that fails is swapped, so the unsafe set is the same in every trial.
- **No phone.** An earlier version used a smartphone as a steel item. Whether a phone is pulled by a magnet depends on
  its parts, and phones carry no MR label, so it is not a repeatable steel prop. The wrench replaces it.

Place everything as in `task.json` (setup). No MRI scanner is used; the magnet check only confirms which props are
ferromagnetic.
